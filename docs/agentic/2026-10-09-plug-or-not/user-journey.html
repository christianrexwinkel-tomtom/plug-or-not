Product concept · user journey

Worth-it charging for plug-in hybrids

A plug-in hybrid driver can always fall back on petrol. So the real question at every charger is: is plugging in here, for as long as I'll stay, cheaper than just driving on petrol? Today no app answers that. Tariffs mix kWh prices, time fees and start fees, and the answer depends on the car, the battery level and the length of the stay.

Who it's for

A plug-in hybrid driver who charges in public

The core idea

Every charger has a "worth-it window"

Instead of a single price, the app tells the driver between which stay lengths charging beats petrol. Below are three real chargers in Eindhoven. Move the slider to change the stay.

2h30

Lower edge: too short

A short stay delivers few kWh, so the €0.89 start fee weighs heavily on each one. At 3.6 kW, a 30-minute stop gives only 1.8 kWh.

Upper edge: too long

Once the battery is full, idle or parking fees keep running while no km are added. Chargers without time fees have no upper edge.

No window at all

High kWh prices, or a time fee on a slow-charging car, mean charging never beats petrol. For a plug-in hybrid at 3.6 kW, €0.41/h works out to an extra €0.11/kWh.

Moment 1 · Planning

"Where should I charge for my meeting at Strijp-S?"

  1. The trip is known

    DriverHas "Workshop, Klokgebouw 50, 14:00–16:30" in the calendar.
    Behind the scenesThe app reads the event's place and time. No typing needed.

  2. The app estimates the stay

    App shows"About 2h30 parked (meeting)". One tap to change.
    Behind the scenesEvent type → typical stay. Calendar end time wins when it's there.

  3. It predicts the battery on arrival

    App shows"You'll arrive with about 12%."
    Behind the scenesVolvo API battery level minus the route's energy use. This sets how many kWh can still go in.

  4. It ranks chargers within walking distance

    App showsEach charger's saving compared with petrol and its worth-it window, best first.
    Behind the scenesNDW OCPI tariffs + card fee + the car's charging speed, for every charger within 600 m.

  5. The driver picks one and drives

    InsightThe charger at the door costs more than petrol. A 5-minute walk away, one saves €2.72.

Moment 2 · Arriving

"I'm parked in Nuenen centre for coffee. Plug in or not?"
